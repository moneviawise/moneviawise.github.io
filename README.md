# Monevia Wise – Planner & Budget Management

This repository powers the marketing site for **Monevia Wise**, a brand focused on simple, calm **budget planners and printable money tools**.

The site is built as a lightweight static website and hosted with **GitHub Pages**, served at:

- **Main site:** https://moneviawise.com  
- **GitHub Pages URL (fallback):** https://moneviawise.github.io

---

## 🌱 What is Monevia Wise?

Monevia Wise creates **digital budget planners, and savings trackers, goal planner and anything printable or digital** that are:

- Beginner-friendly
- Clean and minimal in design
- Easy to print at home or use on a tablet (GoodNotes, etc.)

The goal is to make budgeting feel like a **short check-in**, not a stressful task.

---

## 🧩 Site Structure

The site is organized as a simple multi-page static site:

- `index.html` – Home page / hero, feature overview, and featured planners
- `shop.html` – Grid of digital products that link out to Etsy listings
- `freebies.html` – Free sample printables (PDF downloads)
- `blog.html` – Blog index for site-hosted articles plus preserved Medium archive links
- `blog/posts/` – Website-first blog posts that can be imported into Medium after publishing
- `BLOG_WORKFLOW.md` – Step-by-step workflow for creating site posts and importing them to Medium
- `about.html` – Brand story and values
- `contact.html` – Contact information and demo contact form
- `faq.html` – Common questions about downloads, printing, and usage
- `styles.css` – Shared styles, layout, and color system
- `assets/` – Logos and downloadable files (PDFs, images, etc.)

You can open any of these HTML files directly in a browser for a quick preview.

---

## 🎨 Brand & Design

Core design ideas:

- **Colors**
  - Soft light background
  - Dark navy / near-black text
  - Mint accent for highlights
- **Style**
  - Minimal, calm UI
  - Rounded cards and subtle shadows
  - Focus on readability and white space

All styles live in `styles.css` so layout and appearance can be updated in one place.
